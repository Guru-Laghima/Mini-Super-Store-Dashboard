# Mini Super Store Dashboard

## Project Title
Mini Super Store – Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Objectives
- To analyze the historical sales performance of Mini Super Store across multiple years.
- To identify the highest and lowest performing years, top item categories, and best-performing outlet types.
- To uncover sales trends and patterns that can guide inventory, marketing, and expansion decisions.
- To provide a clear and interactive view of sales performance for data-driven retail strategy.

## Dataset Used
Dataset: Mini Super Store Sales Data  
Contains historical sales records including year, item type, outlet type, outlet size, and sales amount.

## Key Questions Explored
1. What is the total sales revenue generated by Mini Super Store?
2. Which year recorded the highest and lowest sales?
3. How have sales trended over the years (1985–2009)?
4. Which item types contribute the most to total sales?
5. Which outlet types generate the highest revenue?
6. How does sales performance differ across outlet sizes?

## Process
1. **Data Cleaning and Preparation**
   - Standardized item type and outlet type categories.
   - Handled missing or inconsistent values.
   - Aggregated sales by year, item type, and outlet type.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Calculated total sales and identified peak and lowest years.
   - Analyzed year-over-year sales trends.
   - Ranked item categories and outlet types by sales contribution.

## Dashboard Building
- Built an interactive sales dashboard using **Google Looker Studio**.
- Included key performance indicators (Highest Sales Year, Lowest Sales Year, Total Sales).
- Created visualizations for Yearly Sales Trend, Sales by Item Type, Outlet Type distribution (pie chart), and Sales by Outlet Type (bar chart).
- Added filters for Item Type and Outlet Size to allow flexible analysis.

## Recommendations and Insights
1. **Sales Trend**  
   Sales peaked in 1985 (3.6M) and experienced a sharp decline in 1998 (188.3K), followed by partial recovery.  
   ➔ Investigate the cause of the 1998 drop and strengthen strategies to prevent future sharp declines.

2. **Top Performing Categories**  
   Fruits and Vegetables (2.8M) and Snack Foods (2.7M) are the highest contributing item types.  
   ➔ Prioritize inventory availability, promotions, and shelf space for these high-performing categories.

3. **Outlet Type Performance**  
   Supermarket Type1 generates the highest sales (12.9M), significantly outperforming other outlet types.  
   ➔ Focus expansion and resource allocation on Supermarket Type1 format while evaluating the potential of Type3 and Type2.

4. **Grocery Store Opportunity**  
   Grocery Stores contribute the least to total sales (368K).  
   ➔ Assess whether to optimize, rebrand, or reduce investment in the Grocery Store format.

5. **Long-term Stability**  
   After the 1998 dip, sales stabilized between 1.9M and 2.3M in later years.  
   ➔ Build on this relative stability with consistent marketing and product mix strategies.

## Conclusion
This dashboard provides a clear historical view of Mini Super Store’s sales performance. It highlights critical trends, top product categories, and the most profitable outlet types, enabling management to make informed decisions on inventory, marketing, and future expansion. The interactive design allows stakeholders to explore the data easily and extract actionable retail insights.
